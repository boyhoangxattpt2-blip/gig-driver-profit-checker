Assumptions (read this)
  • Payout is the total you receive, tips and bonuses included.
  • Distance must include deadhead — every mile driven for the job, loaded or empty.
  • Minutes include waiting at pickup, traffic, and drop-off. Zero time is not allowed.
  • The vehicle rate is one all-in figure (fuel + maintenance + tires + depreciation). Adding fuel again would double-count.
  • Tolls/parking are subtracted once, separately — never inside the per-mile rate.
  • The verdict compares net hourly pay against your minimum. BORDERLINE means within $2/hr below your bar.
